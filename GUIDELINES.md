Saigon Kids Hackathon Brand Guidelines
This guide establishes the visual identity for Saigon Kids Hackathon, a premier tech event combining a youthful, energetic spirit with the polished, clean aesthetics of collegiate innovation hubs.
1. The Vibe
Core Aesthetic: Clean, energetic, youthful, and tech-focused.


Aesthetic Inspo: Stanford TreeHacks, HackHarvard.


Strict Avoidances:


No animal mascots.


No cluttered multi-color schemes.


No dark cyberpunk or neon styles.


2. Primary Color Palette
The palette is like space themed, the goal is to give that space feeling with the deepspace blue/midnight blue
Color Role Matrix
Deep Space Blue/Midnight Blue: #191970 - Main Canvas / Backdrop
Primary Red: #E63946 / #FF2B2B - Primary Action & Focal Point
Vibrant Yellow: #FFB703 / #FFD166 - Accents & Illumination
Crisp White: #FFFFFF / #E0E1DD - Typography & Highlights


3. Typography
All typography across the brand is consolidated into a single family, using different weights and sizes for hierarchy.
Display Headings & Body Text: DynaPuff (Google Fonts)


Role: Primary Font. Used for everything.


Style: Medium and Bold weights for headlines and key taglines. Regular and Medium weights for all body copy, UI elements, and documentation.


Usage: Keep all typography in sentence case or lowercase to maintain the friendly, approachable, and happy feel.


4. Logo Usage Rules
The circular logo is a core asset and must always be treated with respect to maintain its integrity.
Logo Preservation:


Never stretch or distort its proportions.


Never recolor any element (text, orbits, or pixels).


Never rotate, tilt, or slant the logo mark.


Never apply drop shadows, heavy glows, or gradients.


Placement: Display strictly on the Soft Gray (#F1F5F9) canvas or clean white areas for high contrast.


5. Graphic Language & Visual Elements
Incorporate clean, lightweight vector assets directly inspired by the logo and Stanford TreeHacks:
Pixel Grids: Small block accents in Saigon Blue and Energy Yellow positioned at card corners (e.g., $3 \times 3$ or $2 \times 3$).
